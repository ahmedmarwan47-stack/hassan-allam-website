Overview

Our story began in the 2000s with a vision to create more than buildings, to craft purposeful places and destinations where life unfolds with care, quality, and enduring value.

Anchored by a trusted, family-rooted legacy spanning three generations, HAP draws on over 80 years of development expertise. We blend private luxury with warm hospitality, shaping communities that go far beyond simply building homes.

People Behind HAP

Our Team

Behind every HAP development is a dedicated team of experts; architects, planners, creatives, and visionaries, driven by a shared passion for excellence.

Together, they bring our communities to life with purpose and precision, crafting lasting spaces that reflect our values, enrich lives, and carry forward the legacy we’ve built over generations.

For three generations, the Hassan Allam Properties family has nurtured a proud legacy of shaping exclusive communities where families come together to celebrate life’s most meaningful moments, from lasting unions to new beginnings.

Our communities are thoughtfully built in prime locations, bringing together like-minded neighbors who naturally grow into lifelong friends. These meaningful connections and effortless lifestyles add lasting value, not just to the homes, but to daily life itself. Welcome to Hassan Allam Properties’ circle of trust, where charm, harmony, and a sense of belonging shape everyday living.
